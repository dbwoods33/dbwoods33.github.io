Christmas overspending rarely happens in one big purchase. It’s a gift here, an extra stocking filler there, and a last-minute present for someone you forgot. A gift list with a number next to every name fixes most of it, because you decide before you’re in the shop. This template is that list, with the sums done.

What’s in the free Christmas gift list template

The workbook has a main tab where you do everything and a How to use tab with one-page instructions. Here’s what you get:

  • A total budget at the top: what you can pay for by Christmas without new debt.
  • A gift list with room for 24 gifts: person, gift idea, budget, spent, left and notes. The same person can have several rows.
  • Status for every gift from a drop-down: Idea, Bought or Wrapped, colour-coded so you can see what’s left at a glance.
  • Summary: total budget, what your gift budgets add up to, what you’ve spent and what’s left to spend.
  • A plain-English check that tells you if your gift budgets add up to more than your total, or if you’ve already spent more than you planned.
  • Gift counts: people on the list, gifts on the list, bought or wrapped, and still to buy.

The twelve gifts and the $800 total are sample data, labelled at the top of the sheet. Type over them with your own list; empty rows below are ready for more.

How to open it in Google Sheets

The template is a standard Excel file (.xlsx). Google Sheets converts it in a few seconds, and the formulas, colours and drop-downs carry over.

  1. Download the template to your computer.
  2. Go to drive.google.com, click New → File upload and choose free-christmas-gift-list.xlsx.
  3. Right-click the uploaded file and choose Open with → Google Sheets.
  4. Click File → Save as Google Sheets. This makes a native copy you can edit on your phone and share.

Already have a spreadsheet open? File → Import → Upload works too: pick the file, then choose Insert new sheet(s).

How to use it in Excel

  1. Download the file and double-click to open it in Excel (Windows or Mac, 2016 or newer, or Microsoft 365).
  2. If a yellow Protected View bar appears, click Enable Editing. Excel shows this for every downloaded file; there are no macros in this one.
  3. Use File → Save As to save your own copy.

Prefer Excel online? Upload the file to OneDrive and open it in the browser. It also opens in LibreOffice Calc, which we used to test every formula.

How to use it, in four steps

1. Set the total first

Start from what you can pay for by Christmas without borrowing: savings set aside plus what you can spare from the next few paychecks. Leave room for food, travel and decorations, which aren’t on this list.

2. List everyone, then give each gift a number

Add every person, including the easy-to-forget ones: teachers, neighbours, the work Secret Santa. Then put a budget next to each gift. The summary tells you straight away if the list adds up to more than your total.

3. Record what you actually spend

When you buy something, type the price in Spent and set the status to Bought. Left shows whether you came in under or over (red). Mark it Wrapped when it’s ready.

4. Keep the file for next year

In January, make a copy, clear Spent and set every status back to Idea. Last year’s list and what things really cost is the best starting point you’ll have.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Christmas gift list template really free?

Yes. It’s a direct .xlsx download with no email, account or signup.

How do I use it in Google Sheets?

Download the .xlsx file, upload it to Google Drive, then right-click it and choose Open with → Google Sheets. Choose File → Save as Google Sheets for an editable copy you can share with a partner and update from your phone while shopping.

Does it work in Excel?

Yes, in Excel for Windows and Mac, Excel for the web and LibreOffice Calc. It uses standard formulas (SUM, COUNTIF, COUNTA, SUMPRODUCT, TEXT) and no macros.

Can I list more than one gift per person?

Yes. Use one row per gift and repeat the person’s name. The people count only counts each name once.

Does it include food, travel and decorations?

No, this free list is for gifts. Set your total budget after allowing for those costs. The premium planner has a separate holiday extras budget.

Can I use it for birthdays or other holidays?

Yes. Nothing in the formulas is tied to Christmas: change the title and use it for Hanukkah, Eid, birthdays or a wedding gift list.
