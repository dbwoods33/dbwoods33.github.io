Straight answers about delivery, compatibility and refunds.

How do I get the template after buying?

Checkout and delivery are handled by Gumroad. The download link appears straight after payment and is emailed to you, so you can open the file in under a minute.

Do they work in Google Sheets as well as Excel?

Yes. Every spreadsheet is a standard .xlsx file that works in Excel 2016 or later, Excel for Mac, Microsoft 365 and Google Sheets (File → Import, or just open it from Drive). LibreOffice works too.

Are there macros or add-ins?

No. The templates use ordinary formulas, drop-downs, conditional formatting and charts only, so there are no security prompts and nothing to enable.

Can I edit the categories, colours and labels?

Yes. Categories, accounts and settings live on an editable Settings sheet, and nothing is locked. Each file starts with a short guide and sample data you can clear.

What if it isn’t right for me?

Purchases are processed by Gumroad. If something doesn’t work as described, reply to your receipt email and refunds are handled in line with Gumroad’s refund policy.
