What you get with every free template

We built these to be the version we’d hand a friend: one or two tabs, clear yellow input cells, formulas that do the arithmetic, and a short How to use tab inside the file. Each one opens with sample data and a banner that says so, so you can see the formulas working before you type over them.

  • No signup. The download button is a link to the file. We don’t ask for an email address.
  • Google Sheets and Excel. Standard .xlsx with standard formulas and no macros. Every file is tested in LibreOffice Calc for formula errors before it goes up.
  • Yours to change. Rename categories, add rows, change colours. Nothing is locked.
  • Private. Your numbers stay on your computer or in your own Google Drive.

How to open them in Google Sheets

Download the .xlsx, go to drive.google.com, choose New → File upload, then right-click the file and pick Open with → Google Sheets. Finish with File → Save as Google Sheets to get a native copy you can edit on your phone. In Excel, just double-click the file and click Enable Editing if Protected View appears. Each template page has the full steps.

Which one should you start with?

If you don’t have a budget yet, start with the monthly budget template: everything else is easier once you know where your money goes. Carrying card or loan balances? The debt snowball calculator shows which debt to pay first and when you’ll be free of all of them. Saving for several things at once, like an emergency fund, a holiday and annual bills? Use the savings goal tracker. The habit tracker and Christmas gift list are quick wins for the month ahead.

The free ones are complete for what they do; the premium ones add the parts that take a spreadsheet from a list to a system, such as transaction or payment logs, a full year in one file, dashboards and charts. If the free version covers what you need, keep using it.

Frequently asked questions

Are these spreadsheet templates really free?

Yes. Every template on this page is a direct .xlsx download: no email, no account and no signup. Use them for your own household as often as you like.

Do they work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Download the .xlsx, upload it to Google Drive and choose Open with → Google Sheets, then File → Save as Google Sheets. Formulas, colours and drop-downs carry over.

Do they work in Excel and on a Mac?

Yes. They use standard formulas only and no macros, so they work in Excel for Windows and Mac, Excel for the web and LibreOffice Calc. We test every one in LibreOffice before publishing.

Why is there sample data in the files?

So you can see every formula working before you type anything. Each file has a SAMPLE DATA banner at the top; type over the yellow cells with your own numbers.

What’s the catch?

There isn’t one. Each free template is a simpler version of one of our paid templates. If you outgrow it, the premium version is there; if you don’t, keep using the free one.

Is my data private?

Yes. The files live on your computer or in your own Google Drive. Nothing you type is sent to us.

Need more than the free version?

Every template in the store comes with a dashboard, charts and sample data, for Excel and Google Sheets.
